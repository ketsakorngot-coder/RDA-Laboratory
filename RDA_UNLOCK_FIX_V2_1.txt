RDA Unlock Fix V2.1
===================
Fixed R-02 not unlocking after R-01.
Root cause: strict Decision Record validator required every field to contain >=15 characters, including Risk. Risk values are categorical (LOW/MODERATE/HIGH/CRITICAL), so the validator could never become ready.

New completion rule:
- Native mission completed
- Claim >= 8 chars
- Evidence captured/selected
- Physics Reasoning >= 15 chars
- Risk selected (categorical; non-empty)
- Decision >= 10 chars
- Rule-based Rubric score >= 12/20

The same fix is applied to the master bridge and Headquarters fallback logic, preserving strict sequential unlocking R-01 -> R-06.
