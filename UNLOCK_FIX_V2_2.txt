RDA UNLOCK FIX V2.2

Root cause fixed:
1) R-02 was previously dependent on Decision Quality >= 12/20.
   This could keep R-02 locked even though R-01 had been fully completed.
2) Unlock now depends on:
   - Native mission completed
   - Claim complete
   - Evidence complete
   - Physics Reasoning complete
   - Risk selected
   - Decision complete
   - Revision submitted
   Rubric score is recorded for diagnostics/research but does NOT block progression.
3) Existing completed records are migrated automatically when revisedScore > 0.
4) OPEN_RDA_MASTER.bat now prefers a localhost server so all mission pages
   share the same localStorage origin reliably.

Expected:
R-01 complete + Revision submitted -> R-02 CURRENT MISSION / OPEN
R-02 incomplete -> R-03 LOCKED
