RDA — RADIATION INVESTIGATION & DECISION LABORATORY
==================================================
Core learning architecture:
EVIDENCE → RISK → DECISION

Every case closes with:
CLAIM → EVIDENCE → PHYSICS REASONING → RISK → DECISION

Case architecture (preserves the simulations already built):
R-01 UNKNOWN SOURCE — identify radiation from penetration/detector evidence.
R-02 MEDICAL SHIELD PROTOCOL — choose protection/treatment using evidence.
R-03 SECURITY RISK DECISION — interpret screening evidence and decide Allow/Deny.
R-04 FOOD RADIATION CLAIM — evaluate evidence, public risk, and scientific communication.
R-05 RADIATION INCIDENT — integrate evidence for emergency response.
R-06 NATIONAL DECISION COMMAND — final integrated performance mission.

Implementation:
- Existing simulations, Evidence Vaults, mission progress, XP and unlock logic preserved.
- Shared Decision Lab identity added to every mission.
- FINAL DECISION button added to every R-01..R-06.
- Each mission stores its own 5-part Decision Record in localStorage.
- A record is READY TO DEFEND after all five sections contain substantive responses.
