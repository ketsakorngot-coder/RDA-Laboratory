RDA — RADIATION INVESTIGATION & DECISION LABORATORY
COMPLETE LEARNING & ASSESSMENT ARCHITECTURE
============================================================

CORE: EVIDENCE → RISK → DECISION
EVERY CASE: CLAIM → EVIDENCE → PHYSICS REASONING → RISK → DECISION

R-01 UNKNOWN SOURCE
Physics: Alpha/Beta/Gamma, penetration, attenuation
Decision: identify source + assess risk + immediate handling.

R-02 MEDICAL SHIELD PROTOCOL
Physics: medical radiation, shielding, exposure reduction, safety
Decision: select protection/response measures from evidence.

R-03 SECURITY RISK DECISION
Physics: detection, measurement, penetration, false-positive risk
Decision: allow / isolate / investigate further.

R-04 FOOD RADIATION CLAIM
Physics: radiation applications, dose context, evidence evaluation, risk communication
Decision: judge the claim + communicate public risk.

R-05 RADIATION INCIDENT
Physics: time, distance, shielding, exposure, emergency response
Decision: rescue / evacuation / containment / limited resources.

R-06 NATIONAL DECISION COMMAND
Physics: integrated prior concepts + multi-source evidence
Decision: build and defend a national response plan.

COMPLETION RULE
A case is COMPLETED only when BOTH are true:
1) Native case mission is completed.
2) Final Decision Record is complete (5/5 substantive sections).

DECISION QUALITY RUBRIC — 20 POINTS
Claim 4 | Evidence 4 | Physics Reasoning 4 | Risk 4 | Decision 4
